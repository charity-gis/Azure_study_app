## Suggested six-slide presentation

1. **Problem:** explain a specific concept you personally struggled with and the learner audience.
2. **Solution:** show the dashboard and describe the explain → practise → review → plan loop.
3. **Live demo:** reveal a card, answer a question, show feedback and retry.
4. **Technical choices:** local explanations, shared rules, honest coverage and private optional sync.
5. **Impact:** what the prototype could improve; separate proposed measures from actual results.
6. **Proof and next steps:** 27 passing tests, repository/demo links, known gaps and content expansion.

## Impact measurement plan (not collected results)

- Compare accuracy on a reviewed set before and after a study session.
- Ask learners to explain shared responsibility in their own words.
- Measure return visits and planned sessions completed with informed consent.
- Test whether explanations reduce repeated errors on the same concept.
- Validate usability on small screens and with assistive technology.
